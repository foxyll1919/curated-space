How it works

01

Drop or paste

Drag a screenshot onto the app, paste from clipboard, or pick from your files. Any image works.

02

AI reads & tags

Gemini extracts a title, description, and tags from each screenshot — instantly and privately.

03

Search anything

Find any screenshot by what's in it. Semantic search means you don't need to remember exact words.

Ask your library

Use natural language to ask questions about everything you've saved. Curated Space uses RAG to find relevant screenshots and answer from your personal collection. Like having a conversation with your visual memory.

You
"What color palette did I use in that dark-mode dashboard?"
