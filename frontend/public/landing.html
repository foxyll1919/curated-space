A note on AI and your screenshots

Curated Space uses Google Gemini to read your screenshots and extract titles, descriptions and tags. Screenshots are sent to the Gemini API for analysis only — they are not used to train models. You can save any screenshot without analysis by toggling "Don't analyze," and it will never be sent to any AI provider. Review Google's Gemini API terms and billing tiers for complete data-handling details.
